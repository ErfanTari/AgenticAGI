# artisan-bakery-spec


## Artisan Bakery Website Specification

A high-end, single-file HTML website for "L'Artisan Boulangerie," a neighborhood artisan bakery.

### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Style:** Rustic elegance, cozy, warm, and artisanal.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#fdfaf5` (Warm Cream)
  - Primary: `#4e342e` (Deep Espresso)
  - Secondary: `#8d6e63` (Soft Cocoa)
  - Accent: `#d87d4a` (Warm Terracotta)
  - Text: `#2c2c2c` (Charcoal)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: 'Playfair Display', serif (elegant, classic)
  - Body: 'Lato', sans-serif (clean, readable)

### Structure & Sections
1. **Navigation:** Sticky, transparent-to-solid transition, links to About, Menu, Gallery, Contact.
2. **Hero Section:** Full-height immersive section with a high-quality placeholder image (Unsplash), a poetic headline (e.g., "Handcrafted Daily, Shared with Love"), and a "View Today's Bake" CTA.
3. **About Section:** Two-column layout. One side with a rustic image, the other with a story about sourdough, heritage grains, and community.
4. **Menu Section:** 
   - Categorized tabs (Breads, Pastries, Brews).
   - Elegant list items with name, description, and price.
   - Hover effects on menu items.
5. **Gallery Section:** A masonry or clean grid layout showcasing close-up shots of textures (flour, crust, steam).
6. **Testimonials:** A soft-styled slider or grid of customer quotes.
7. **Contact & Hours:** 
   - Left: Contact form (Name, Email, Message).
   - Right: Opening hours, Address, and a stylized Map placeholder.
8. **Footer:** Social icons (Instagram, Facebook), newsletter signup, and copyright.

### Technical Requirements
- **Single File:** All CSS and JS must be inline.
- **Libraries (via CDN):**
  - Google Fonts: Playfair Display & Lato.
  - FontAwesome: For social and UI icons.
  - AOS (Animate On Scroll): For elegant fade-in/slide-up effects.
- **Interactivity:**
  - Smooth scrolling for navigation.
  - Tab switching for the menu.
  - Form validation (client-side).
  - Mobile-responsive hamburger menu.
  - AOS animations on scroll.
- **Images:** Use high-quality Unsplash URLs for placeholders (e.g., bread, pastries, cafe interior).
