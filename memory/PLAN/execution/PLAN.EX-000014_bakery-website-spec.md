# bakery-website-spec


## Artisan Bakery Website Specification

### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Color Palette:** Warm, organic tones. Cream (#FDFBF7) for background, Deep Cocoa (#3E2723) for text, Sage Green (#8F9779) for accents, and Golden Wheat (#D4A373) for highlights.
- **Typography:** 
  - Headings: 'Playfair Display' (Serif) for an elegant, traditional feel.
  - Body: 'Lato' or 'Montserrat' (Sans-serif) for readability.
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist but warm, high-quality imagery, plenty of whitespace, soft shadows, and organic shapes.

### Structure & Sections
1.  **Navigation:** Sticky header with logo (text-based), Menu, Our Story, Gallery, Location, and a "Order Now" CTA button.
2.  **Hero Section:** Large, high-impact image of fresh bread/pastries, an inviting headline ("Handcrafted Daily, Shared Locally"), and a primary CTA.
3.  **Our Story:** A section describing the artisan process, local sourcing, and the bakery's heart.
4.  **Featured Menu:** A grid of cards showing categories (Sourdough, Pastries, Seasonal) with prices and descriptions.
5.  **Gallery:** A masonry or grid layout of beautiful bakery shots.
6.  **Location & Hours:** A clean section with an embedded-style map (using a styled div/placeholder), address, and a clear table of operating hours.
7.  **Footer:** Social links, newsletter signup (visual only), and copyright.

### Technical Requirements
- **Single File:** All CSS and JS must be inline within `index.html`.
- **Libraries:** 
  - Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Lato).
  - Lucide Icons (via CDN) for iconography.
  - AOS (Animate On Scroll) or simple Intersection Observer for fade-in effects.
- **Interactivity:**
  - Smooth scrolling for nav links.
  - Mobile-responsive hamburger menu.
  - Hover effects on menu items and buttons.
  - Simple form validation for the contact section.
- **Images:** Use high-quality Unsplash URLs for placeholders (e.g., bread, flour, coffee, bakery interior).
