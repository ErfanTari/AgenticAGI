The Studio

Lumina is an architectural design firm focused on the intersection of space, light, and material. We believe that architecture is not just about buildings, but about how those structures influence human behavior and emotion.

Founded in 2015, our team of architects and designers works collaboratively to create environments that are both aesthetically striking and functionally superior. From residential renovations to commercial spaces, we approach every project with a unique perspective tailored to the client's vision.
