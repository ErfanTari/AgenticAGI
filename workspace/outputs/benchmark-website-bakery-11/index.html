Our Story

From Grain to Golden Crust

Founded in 2015, Artisan Crust began with a simple mission: to bring the honest, slow-fermented flavors of traditional sourdough back to our community.

We source our organic flour from local mills and work closely with seasonal farmers to ensure every ingredient tells a story of the land.
