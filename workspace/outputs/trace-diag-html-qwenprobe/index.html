Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Golden Crumb began as a small kitchen dream. We believe that bread is more than just food—it's a tradition, a community builder, and a source of joy.


We use only locally sourced organic flour, pure butter, and natural ingredients. No preservatives, no shortcuts. Just time, patience, and passion poured into every loaf.
