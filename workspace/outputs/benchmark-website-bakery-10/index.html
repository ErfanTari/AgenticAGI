Our Story

Founded in a small kitchen with nothing but flour, water, and salt, The Golden Crust was born from a desire to return to the roots of baking. We believe that great bread cannot be rushed.


We source our grains from local organic farmers and use traditional long-fermentation methods. This not only develops a complex flavor profile but also makes our bread easier to digest and more nutritious.
