Our Story

Welcome to The Daily Crumb, where every loaf tells a tale and every pastry holds a memory. Founded by a passionate baker with a dream to bring authentic, handcrafted goods to our neighborhood, we believe in the simple magic of flour, water, salt, and yeast.

Our journey began in a small kitchen, experimenting with age-old techniques and sourcing the finest local ingredients. Today, we continue that tradition, baking fresh daily with no shortcuts, just pure dedication. From our slow-fermented sourdoughs to our flaky croissants, each item is a testament to our commitment to quality and flavor. We're more than just a bakery; we're a community hub, a place where friends gather, and the aroma of fresh bread fills the air. Come, be a part of our story.
