Our Story

At The Daily Crumb, we believe in the magic of traditional baking. Our journey began with a simple passion: to bring authentic, handcrafted baked goods to our community. Using only the finest ingredients, time-honored techniques, and a dash of creativity, we bake everything from scratch, ensuring every bite is a moment of pure delight. We're more than just a bakery; we're a place where friends gather, stories are shared, and the simple pleasure of a perfect pastry brightens your day.

From our flaky croissants to our rustic sourdough, each item tells a tale of dedication and artistry. We source local ingredients whenever possible, supporting our neighbors and ensuring the freshest flavors. Come visit us and taste the difference that true artisan baking makes!
