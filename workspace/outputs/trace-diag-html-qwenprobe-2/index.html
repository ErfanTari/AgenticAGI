Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Golden Crumb began with a simple mission: to bring the art of traditional baking back to our community. We believe that bread is more than just food—it's a connection to history and a celebration of craftsmanship.

  • Locally sourced organic grains
  • Wood-fired ovens
  • No preservatives, just time and patience
