A Tradition of Taste

Founded in 1985 by Elena Rossi, The Golden Crumb began as a small family kitchen with a big dream: to bring authentic European baking techniques to our local community.

We believe in slow fermentation, stone-ground flours, and ingredients sourced from local farmers. Every loaf we bake is a testament to patience and craftsmanship.
