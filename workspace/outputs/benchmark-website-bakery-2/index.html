Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Golden Crumb began as a small family dream to bring authentic European baking techniques to our neighborhood. We believe that great bread is made with patience, quality ingredients, and love.

Every morning at 4 AM, our bakers are up and kneading, ensuring that when you walk in, the smell of fresh yeast and caramelized crust fills the air. We source our flour from local mills and use only organic butter and eggs.
