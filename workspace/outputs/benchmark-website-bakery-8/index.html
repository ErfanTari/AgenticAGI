Our Story

Founded in 2018, Crumb & Hearth began with a simple mission: to bring the warmth of neighborhood baking back to our community. We believe that bread is more than just food—it's a gathering place, a comfort, and a craft.

Our bakers wake up before dawn every day to mix, knead, and bake using traditional methods. We source our flour from local mills and use only natural leavening agents.
