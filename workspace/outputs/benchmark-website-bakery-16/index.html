Our Story

Founded in 2015, The Golden Crust began as a humble dream to bring authentic, small-batch baking back to Willowbrook. We believe that great bread starts with great ingredients, which is why we source 100% organic flour, locally grown grains, and naturally leavened starters.

Every loaf is hand-shaped, slow-fermented, and baked in our stone hearth ovens. We don't rush perfection. Our commitment to traditional methods ensures that every bite carries the warmth of craftsmanship and the joy of community.
