Our Story

Founded in 2010, The Daily Crumb began as a small kitchen experiment with a simple goal: to bring the authentic taste of traditional European baking back to our neighborhood. We believe that bread is more than just food; it's a connection to history and community.

We use only locally sourced, organic ingredients and time-honored fermentation techniques. Every loaf is hand-shaped with care, ensuring that the texture and flavor are perfect for your table.
