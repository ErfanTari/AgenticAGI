Our Story

Founded in 1985, The Golden Crumb has been a family-owned sanctuary for those who appreciate the art of baking. We believe that great bread starts with quality ingredients.

We use traditional sourdough methods, allowing our dough to ferment naturally for 48 hours. Every loaf is baked fresh daily using locally sourced grains and organic butter.

Our mission is to bring the warmth of traditional European baking to your neighborhood, one golden crumb at a time.
