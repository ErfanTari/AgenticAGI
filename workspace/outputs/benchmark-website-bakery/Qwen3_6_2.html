Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Golden Crust began as a small family kitchen with a big dream: to bring authentic, slow-fermented breads and handcrafted pastries back to our community. Every morning, our bakers arrive before dawn to mix, fold, and bake using time-honored techniques.

We source organic flour, farm-fresh eggs, and locally grown herbs. No shortcuts, just pure flavor and the comforting aroma of a true bakery.
