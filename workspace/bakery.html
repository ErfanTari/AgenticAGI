Our Story

At The Daily Crumb, we believe that bread is more than just food—it's a tradition. Founded in 2015, our bakery has been dedicated to reviving the art of sourdough and pastry making using time-honored techniques.


We source only the finest organic grains, locally grown fruits, and pure butter. Our commitment is to the community, ensuring that every loaf we pull from the oven brings warmth and joy to your table.
