# Project: Block Brawlers: Streets of Pixels

## 1. Game Overview

"Block Brawlers: Streets of Pixels" is a 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up that combines the classic brawling mechanics and intensity of the "Streets of Rage" series with the iconic blocky, pixelated aesthetic of "Minecraft". Players will traverse a block-rendered city, battling a criminal syndicate using a variety of melee attacks, special moves, and environmental interactions, all while enjoying a distinct visual style.

## 2. Core Game Mechanics

*   **Genre:** 2D Side-Scrolling Beat 'em Up.
*   **Gameplay Loop:** Players move from left to right through linear stages, defeating waves of enemies, mini-bosses, and ultimately a stage boss.
*   **Combat System:**
    *   **Melee Focus:** Primary combat involves punches, kicks, and throws.
    *   **Combos:** Chaining basic attacks together for increased damage and score.
    *   **Grabs & Throws:** Players can grab certain enemies and throw them into other enemies or environmental hazards.
    *   **Special Moves:** Each character possesses unique, powerful special attacks that consume a portion of their health or a limited "special meter."
    *   **Weapons:** Temporary weapons (e.g., wooden planks, pickaxes, TNT blocks) can be picked up from defeated enemies or environmental objects.
*   **Player Count:** 1-2 players (local or online co-op).
*   **Health System:** Each character has a health bar. Health pickups (e.g., pixelated chicken, apples) can be found in levels.
*   **Scoring System:** Points awarded for defeating enemies, performing combos, and collecting items. High scores encourage replayability.
